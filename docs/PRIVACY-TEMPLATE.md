# Privacy policy outline (not legal advice; have a lawyer review)
1. **What we collect:** email, name, date of birth, gender and preferences, country, messages, and a live selfie taken at signup.
2. **Why:** matching by age and preference, age and safety checks, moderation, and keeping the service secure.
3. **Selfie:** captured in the app only, used to check your age, visible only to authorised reviewers, deleted after review (state your actual retention period). It is never shown on your profile.
4. **Sharing:** your country and other details are shown to another user only when you both agree. We do not sell personal data.
5. **Sensitive data:** the rainbow flag reflects sexual orientation; it is optional and you can remove it any time.
6. **Under 18:** users aged 13-17 are matched only with similar-age users and cannot enable adult content. Describe parental consent rules for your regions.
7. **Your rights:** access, correction, deletion, and withdrawing consent. Give a contact email.
8. **Security and reporting:** how you protect data, how long you keep messages and reports, and how users report abuse.
